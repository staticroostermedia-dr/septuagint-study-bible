# Source and OCR notes

This file lists places where the printed Harper & Weidner lesson is hard to read in the OCR, or where the Gallicantus app’s NA28 Greek / trimmed WEB English differs from the World English Bible, from the Textus Receptus, or from an earlier draft of this workbook that used the SBLGNT. The teaching follows the app’s Greek. English follows the Gallicantus WEB as already trimmed to the Greek; where a trim is not yet available, the lesson follows the Greek, not a TR or untrimmed WEB addition.

Greek text: STEPBible TAGNT (Tyndale House, CC BY 4.0), following NA28 wording, as used in the Gallicantus app. English is the World English Bible (public domain), trimmed to the Greek. Harper & Weidner 1916 is public domain. Harper’s own printed Greek in Part II of the 1916 book is a Westcott-Hort / Tregelles / Tischendorf text, not NA28.

On 27 Nov 2026 (America/Los_Angeles) the app’s NT tokens were corrected (local next-batch commit 605c4af1): 64 NA28 words that had been missing in 59 verses were restored from TAGNT’s variant column. The eight John verses in that list are 7:53 and 8:3, 8:4, 8:5, 8:6, 8:7, 8:9, and 8:10 (ἐπορεύθησαν, ἐπὶ, κατείληπται, λιθάζειν, κατέγραφεν, ἀνέκυψεν, αὐτοῖς, οὖσα, Γύναι). The workbook Greek for John was regenerated from those fixed app tokens, with glosses and parsing for the restored forms.

## Drake’s rule (27 Nov 2026, America/Los_Angeles)

If a passage is in our Greek text, show it plainly with its English: no brackets, no “later-attested” notes, and no textual-criticism flags on the learner’s screen. Those flags belong in this file only. In-lesson callouts and practice prompts that contrasted NA28 with the WEB, SBLGNT, or later/TR readings — including the flags on John 7:53–8:11 in Lessons 37, 40, and 50 — have been removed from the workbook screens. Lesson 25’s callout on the OCR of Harper’s heading (John 3:1–10, not “ii. 1–10”) remains, because it is a note on the printed lesson heading, not a biblical textual flag.

Workbook English removes only words with no NA28 basis. A first pass over-trimmed John 7:53–8:11 by following an incomplete token file; those NA28-backed English words have been restored, and the Greek tokens have now been regenerated from the corrected app data. 8:8 keeps “wrote on the ground” and does not add “with his finger,” which is not in NA28 8:8 (ἔγραφεν εἰς τὴν γῆν).

- **John 1:51.** “hereafter” dropped (NA28 has no ἀπ᾽ ἄρτι here). The app’s WEB line still has it.
- **John 7:53.** “Everyone went to his own house,” — “went” restored (NA28 ἐπορεύθησαν).
- **John 8:5.** “about her” remains absent (not in NA28). “to stone” is kept (NA28 λιθάζειν).
- **John 8:6.** “wrote on the ground with his finger” restored (NA28 τῷ δακτύλῳ κατέγραφεν εἰς τὴν γῆν).
- **John 8:7.** “he looked up and said to them” restored (NA28 ἀνέκυψεν καὶ εἶπεν αὐτοῖς).
- **John 8:8.** “Again he stooped down and wrote on the ground.” (NA28 ἔγραφεν εἰς τὴν γῆν; no τῷ δακτύλῳ). The app’s WEB line still adds “with his finger.”
- **John 8:9.** “being convicted by their conscience” and “even to the last” remain absent (not in NA28). Reviewed English: “They, when they heard it, went out one by one, beginning from the oldest. He was left alone with the woman where she was, in the middle.”
- **John 8:10.** “Woman,” restored (NA28 γύναι). “saw her” and “your accusers” remain absent. Reviewed English: “Jesus, standing up, said to her, “Woman, where are they? Did no one condemn you?””
- **John 21:25.** No closing “Amen” (none in the app or in NA28 here).
- **John 5:4** remains absent, with no in-lesson note.

The app’s WEB English still has “only born Son” at 1:18, “son of Jonah” at 1:42 and 21:15–17, and “Bethesda” at 5:2. Those lines are left as the app has them in the reread English; the difference is recorded only here, not on the lesson screens.


## Lessons 1–10

- **John 1:18.** NA28 (like SBLGNT) reads μονογενὴς θεός, “only-begotten God.” The app’s WEB English still has “only born Son.” Lessons teach the Greek reading and do not treat the two as the same line.
- **ἐρχόμενον in 1:9.** Harper has a footnote on whether the participle goes with φῶς or with ἄνθρωπον. The lesson flags the point rather than inventing a decision Harper does not make clearly in the OCR.
- **English-to-Greek exercises.** Where Harper’s printed Greek key is messy in the OCR, the tiles were composed from his English and from forms already taught, not guessed from a damaged line. Those items should be checked against a clear page image before a typeset edition.
- **Spelling in Lessons 1–10.** Those lessons were first taken from the Cursor Page, which used SBLGNT word forms. SBLGNT and NA28 agree at the points those lessons teach, including 1:18. Some punctuation and elision marks may still use the Page’s U+02BC (ʼ) rather than the app’s U+1FBD Greek coronis (᾽), and capitalisation or accent detail may still follow the Page (for example ἓν / ἕν, Θεόν / θεόν). Later lessons use the app’s NA28 tokens.

## Lessons 11–20

- **English-to-Greek tiles** in these lessons are likewise composed from Harper’s English sentences and from forms already taught, because the OCR of his Greek keys is often unreliable. Flagged as doubtful until checked against a clear scan: Lesson 11 “Art thou the prophet?” / “He said to him, Who art thou?”; Lesson 12 “They were in the desert.” / “He makes straight the way of the prophet.”; Lesson 13 “Ye do not know them, but I know them.”; Lesson 14 “I knew this man.” (capital ᾜδειν); Lesson 15 “I saw the son of man coming from heaven.”; and the similar short sentences in 16–19.
- **Lesson 11 chart.** The first-aorist paradigms of ἐρωτάω, ἀρνέομαι, and ἀποκρίνομαι follow the usual printed paradigms. Harper’s own accent and ending detail should be checked against a clear scan of Vocabulary B.
- **John 1:27.** The app’s English is already trimmed to the Greek (it does not add “before me”).
- **John 1:42.** NA28 reads ὁ υἱὸς Ἰωάννου, “the son of John.” The app’s WEB English still has “son of Jonah.” The lesson follows the Greek.
- **John 1:51.** The app’s WEB English still has “hereafter.” NA28 does not have ἀπ᾽ ἄρτι (or a similar “hereafter”) in this verse; that word belongs to some later or TR-type texts. The workbook English drops “hereafter.”
- **Punctuation in the app tokens.** Many NA28 verse records in the app are stored as words without comma or question-mark tokens. The Read step therefore shows a simple space between words and a period at the end of the verse. Sense-pauses are taught in the pattern lines and in the English.

## Lessons 32–40

- **English-to-Greek tiles** are again composed from Harper’s English and from forms already taught, because the OCR of his Greek keys is often unreliable. Check against a clear scan before a typeset edition.
- **John 5:4** is absent from NA28 and from the app (the English of 5:3 is already trimmed). Lesson 32 omits it; the reviews in Lessons 40 and 50 do the same, with no in-lesson note.
- **John 5:2.** NA28 and the app read Βηθζαθά. The Gallicantus WEB English still has “Bethesda.” Lessons 32 and 40 follow the Greek for the name of the pool.
- **John 5:30.** The app’s English is already trimmed to τοῦ πέμψαντός με, “him who sent me,” and does not add “my Father.”
- **John 7:49.** The Greek has a collective subject with a plural predicate, ἐπάρατοί εἰσιν, “they are cursed.” The app’s English has “is cursed,” which follows English idiom and hides the plural. Lesson 37 teaches the Greek.
- **John 7:53.** NA28 / TAGNT reads Καὶ ἐπορεύθησαν ἕκαστος εἰς τὸν οἶκον αὐτοῦ. The workbook English is “Everyone went to his own house.” The restored token ἐπορεύθησαν is now in the Read step.
- **John 8:7.** NA28 / TAGNT reads ἀνέκυψεν καὶ εἶπεν αὐτοῖς. The workbook English is “he looked up and said to them.” The restored tokens ἀνέκυψεν and αὐτοῖς are now in the Read step.
- **John 7:53–8:11** is in the app in NA28 double brackets (later attested). Lessons 37, 40, and 50 include the span and show it plainly, with no on-screen flag. See Drake’s rule above for the English trims in this span.
- **Suspended nominative (Lesson 38).** Harper’s observation names the construction and cites verse numbers; the OCR of those references is worn (vii. 385 / viii. 40 in the extract). The lesson teaches the construction and flags the references as doubtful until checked against a clear scan.
- **Review 40.** The Read step presents John 5:1–9:41 verse by verse (270 verses, 5:4 omitted). That is a long Read, as Harper’s review text requires.

## Lessons 41–50

- **English-to-Greek tiles** are again composed from Harper’s English and from forms already taught, because the OCR of his Greek keys is often unreliable. Check against a clear scan before a typeset edition.
- **John 10:38.** The Greek is ἵνα γνῶτε καὶ γινώσκητε, that you may come to know (aorist) and may know (present). Harper notes the two tenses on purpose. The app’s English has “that you may know and believe,” which flattens γινώσκητε. Lesson 41 follows the Greek.
- **πιστεύω with ἐν (3:15).** Harper lists a possible ἐν with the dative as a very rare construction, and asks the student to verify it; he notes only one undisputed New Testament example (Mark 1:15). Lesson 41 mentions the point and does not treat ἐν as the ordinary construction of πιστεύω.
- **ἀκούω verse references (Lesson 41).** The OCR of Harper’s list of examples for the genitive and accusative after ἀκούω is badly worn. The constructions themselves are taught from clear verses such as 10:27 (τῆς φωνῆς μου ἀκούουσιν).
- **Middle-voice examples (Lesson 42).** Harper distinguishes direct, indirect, and subjective middles, and cites forms in chapter 11. Particular identifications should be checked against a clear scan when a given form is in doubt.
- **ὅπως in John.** Harper notes that ὅπως occurs only once in John (11:57). Lesson 43 records that fact with Principle 52.
- **Recitative ὅτι (Lesson 45).** Harper’s list of verse numbers for quotation with or without ὅτι is crowded in the OCR. The description of the construction (direct speech, with ὅτι sometimes redundant) is clear and is what the lesson teaches.
- **Principle 57 example (Lesson 46).** One of Harper’s example references in the OCR is worn (it appears as “vii. 9” for a line from the prayer in chapter 17). The construction is taught from 17:9, περὶ ὧν δέδωκάς μοι.
- **John 19:25.** Words of kinship are often omitted before a genitive of relationship. The exact relationships in 19:25 should be checked against a clear scan of Harper’s note and of the printed text.
- **John 21:15–17.** NA28 and the app read Σίμων Ἰωάννου, Simon son of John (as at 1:42). The Gallicantus WEB English still has “son of Jonah.” Lessons 49 and 50 follow the Greek.
- **Review 50.** The Read step presents John 1:1–21:25 verse by verse (878 verses; 5:4 omitted, with no in-lesson note). 7:53–8:11 is included and shown plainly. That is a very long Read, as Harper’s review of the whole Gospel requires.
